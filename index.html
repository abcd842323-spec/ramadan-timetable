Sehri & Iftar duas

Sehri ki niyyat (intention for the fast)

وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَّوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ

Wa bi-sawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri Ramadan.

Meaning: "I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan." Niyyat dil se hoti hai — ye alfaaz sirf usi irade ko zubaan par late hain.

Iftar ki dua (breaking the fast)

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَعَلَىٰ رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ

Allahumma inni laka sumtu, wa bika aamantu, wa 'alayka tawakkaltu, wa 'ala rizqika aftartu.

Meaning: "O Allah, I fasted for You, I believe in You, I trust in You, and with Your provision I break my fast."

Important notes · zaroor samjhein

Dates har saal ~11 din pehle kyun ho jati hain?

Islamic (Hijri) saal chaand par chalta hai — 354 ya 355 din ka — jabki Gregorian saal 365 din ka hota hai. Is ~11 din ke farq ki wajah se Ramadan har saal peeche khisakta rehta hai: 2026 me 19 Feb → 2027 me 8 Feb → 2028 me ~28 Jan. Isi liye purana timetable kabhi dobara istemal na karein — har saal naya timetable check karna zaroori hai.

Moon sighting — ek din ka farq pad sakta hai

Upar ki dates Umm al-Qura calendar (Saudi Arabia ka official civil calendar) par based hain: pehla Roza 8 Feb 2027, aakhri Roza 8 Mar 2027, Eid al-Fitr 9 Mar 2027. India, Pakistan aur Bangladesh jaise deshon me Ramadan local chaand dikhne se shuru hota hai, isliye wahan shuruaat ek din baad (9 Feb) ho sakti hai — upar "+1 day" wala option isiliye diya hai. Apne sheher ki final date local moon-sighting committee / masjid se confirm karein.

Times kaise nikale gaye hain?

  • Har sheher ke latitude/longitude se suraj ki position calculate karke Fajr (Sehri end) aur Maghrib (Iftar) nikala gaya hai — yehi astronomical method bade prayer-time calendars use karte hain.
  • Method badalne (Karachi / MWL / Umm al-Qura / Egypt / ISNA) se Fajr ke time me kuch minute ka farq pad sakta hai. Apni masjid jis method ko follow karti hai, wahi chunein.
  • Kisi khaas din masjid ke printed calendar se 2–5 minute ka farq mile to masjid wala time hi follow karein — local calendar ehtiyat (safety margin) jod sakta hai.
